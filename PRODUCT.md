# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web: a static portfolio published on GitHub Pages at tyuityuitddd.github.io.

## Users
People discovering TDDD's art, prospective commissioning clients, and game or design collaborators. Chinese, English, and Japanese readers.

## Product Purpose
Let visitors explore the creator's illustrations, game projects, and design work, follow their Godot development journal, and make contact.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse image-rich work on desktop and phones. Existing images are optimized WebP; videos are externally hosted. Hosting should remain free.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve portfolio categories, work detail pages, language switching, the development journal, contact links, and the current upload/organizing CMS configuration. Commissioned illustrations display without filenames or titles. Do not fabricate project history or client claims. Journal writing uses the creator's natural first-person voice.

## Brand Commitments
Public identity: TDDD / 泰迪迪迪. The creator's own artwork is the primary visual evidence. The user explicitly wants a more distinctive redesign, with mouse response and different scroll transitions, and has selected a full visual proposal before implementation. Keep motion optional and accessible.

## Evidence on Hand
Current source, content/settings.json, content/works, journal content, supplied artwork in public/media, and the existing live site. Current implementation is evidence of functionality, not a visual authority for the redesign.

## Product Principles
Artwork earns attention before decorative interface. Exploration stays understandable across screen sizes. Animation supports the work and never blocks reading or navigation. Updating content should not require redesigning pages.
